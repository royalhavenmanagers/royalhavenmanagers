## Key Features

### Landlord Rental Income Calculator
Located at `#calculator`, this tool lets property owners estimate their net rental earnings in seconds.
- Multi-currency support: NGN, USD, GBP, and CAD
- Sliders for units (1 to 24) and annual rent
- Quick presets for luxury flats, duplexes, blocks of flats, and estates
- Transparent breakdown showing 90% direct owner payout and 10% management fee
- Clear comparison between managing alone and letting Royal Haven manage
- One-click button that pre-fills the inquiry modal with the calculated numbers

### Property Owner Portal
Located at `#portal`, this provides a secure dashboard for registered landlords.
- Overview cards for occupancy, gross rent, net payouts, and maintenance
- Multi-property selection
- Remittance statements with download and print options
- Routine inspection reports with photo evidence
- Document vault for title deeds, tenancy agreements, and survey plans

### Staff Admin Portal
Located at `#admin`, this gives management complete operational control.
- Property and tenant directory
- Remittance creation and payout tracking
- Confirmation prompts before deleting properties, remittances, documents, or inspections
- Automated deduplication so properties are never doubled
- Inquiries inbox for website leads

### SEO and Google Visibility
- Google FAQPage schema answering top landlord questions directly on search results
- Local search optimization for Lekki, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Ikeja, Magodo, and Abeokuta
- Updated sitemap indexing the income calculator

### Knowledge Hub and Social Sharing
- Real estate guides and market updates
- Social preview cards with cover images when links are shared on WhatsApp, Twitter, or LinkedIn
- Direct link copy with instant confirmation

### Visitor Traffic Analytics
- Daily pageview counts (today, yesterday, past week, and all-time)
- 14-day traffic chart in the admin portal
