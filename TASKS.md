### 1. Google Search Console and Sitemap
Submit your sitemap so Google can index your new pages and show the FAQ accordion snippets.
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console
2. In the top left dropdown, select Add Property
3. Choose URL prefix and enter https://www.royalhaven.com.ng/
4. On the left menu, click Sitemaps
5. In the box, type sitemap.xml and click Submit
6. To confirm Google sees your FAQ schema, test your homepage at https://search.google.com/test/rich-results

### 3. Custom Domain Email Setup (Optional)
If you want automatic confirmation emails sent from your own domain rather than a generic address:
1. Open https://resend.com/domains
2. Add royalhaven.com.ng
3. Add the three DNS records provided by Resend to your domain registrar
4. Update RESEND_SENDER_EMAIL in Vercel to inquiries@royalhaven.com.ng
